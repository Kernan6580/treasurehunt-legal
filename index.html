Know what you're holding.

TreasureHunt is a thrift scanner and resale map for people who buy to resell. Scan a find in the aisle, see what it is and what to check, then plan your next run.

A few photos. The details that matter.

Snap the item, then its labels, barcode or maker's mark. TreasureHunt reads barcodes and labels right on your iPhone, then identifies the item and tells you what that identification is based on.

What it is, and how sure

Each result says what it matched on: a barcode, a model label, a maker's mark, or looks alone. When it isn't sure, it asks for the one close-up that would settle it.

What to check before you buy

Does the shutter fire, is the lens hazy, is the lid chipped. You get the checks a photo can't answer, specific to the item.

No made-up prices

TreasureHunt doesn't guess a price. You get the item, the checks, one-tap searches of eBay sold listings and other marketplaces, and a calculator that turns the real prices you find into your max buy, net and ROI.

Where do my photos go?

Scans are saved on your iPhone. When you ask TreasureHunt to identify an item, a smaller copy of up to four photos, plus any barcode or label text, goes to our server and on to Anthropic's Claude to identify it. We don't keep the photos on our server. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it tell me what something is worth?

It tells you what the item is, what that's based on, and what to check, then takes you straight to sold listings on eBay, Poshmark and Mercari so you can see real prices. Enter what you expect it to sell for and TreasureHunt works out your max buy, net and ROI. It doesn't invent a price.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Scans save offline and identify on their own when you're back online. The map and route need a connection to load stores.

A store is missing or closed. How do I fix it?

On the map, tap the plus button and choose "Add a missing store", or open a store and tap Report. Reports from two different people hide a closed store for everyone.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I delete my account?

Use the delete option in Account & settings on the Pro tab, or email the address on this page and we'll delete it for you.
